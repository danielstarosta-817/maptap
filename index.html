    Does going late help?

    909 scores posted on the day

    The theory in the chat is that whoever plays after dinner — or just not at 7am before coffee — has the advantage. It isn't true. Across 909 same-day posts the correlation between posting time and score is −0.006: nothing. Five of the seven players score slightly worse on their own later days, and the highest win rate in the group belongs to the people posting before 7am.
